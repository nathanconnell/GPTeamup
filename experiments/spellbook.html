Example formula

Read idea and constraints. Identify one uncertainty worth testing. Propose the smallest useful experiment using available materials. Give steps, observations to record, and criteria for interpreting the result. State assumptions and leave final design decisions to the user.

Your first lobspell

One paste is enough to start.

  1. Choose a spell under Use spells.
  2. Fill its inputs, or choose Try sample inputs.
  3. Choose Copy full handoff and paste into an arty conversation.
  4. The arty reads the induction and formula, then does the requested work.

In that same prepared conversation you can later use compact lobspells. After changing books or definitions, send a full handoff again so the arty has the matching revision.

Does this page use AI?The editor, forms, and exports are ordinary browser code. AI is used when you send the prepared text to your arty.
Can it make new spells and books?Yes. Write and save a spell here, or import a definition returned by an arty. Organize those spells into books and export them.
Does saving change the public website?No. Your workspace is personal. Publishing a book to the shared GitHub collection is a separate step.
What are induction and formulae?The induction explains how the arty reads a lobspell. A formula explains how it fulfills one particular spell. The spellbook stores those definitions; the lobspell supplies the particular inputs.
